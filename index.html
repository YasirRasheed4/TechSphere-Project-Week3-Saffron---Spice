Our Story

Founded in 2024 in the heart of Lahore, Saffron & Spice began with a simple mission: bringing authentic, traditional home-cooked flavors back to busy city life. Every dish is crafted using fresh local ingredients, cold-pressed oils, and generational spice blends passed down through our family.

We pride ourselves on non-commercial cooking, ensuring each order feels like a meal prepared right in your mother's kitchen. Cleanliness, authenticity, and rich heritage are packed into every box we ship.
